Frequently asked questions

MRO Pro FAQ
What is MRO management software?

MRO (Maintenance, Repair, and Operations) management software helps factories track the spare parts, tools, and consumables needed to keep production equipment running. MRO Pro combines inventory, work-order, and procurement management so maintenance teams reduce unplanned downtime and avoid both stockouts and overstock.

Is MRO Pro cloud-based or desktop?

MRO Pro is an offline-first desktop application (Windows, macOS, Linux). Your data stays on your machine, which suits plants with restricted internet access or strict data-residency requirements. A machine-bound license key unlocks the full version after the 14-day trial.

How does the trial and license work?

Every install includes a 14-day full-feature trial. After the trial you register a license key in the format MROPRO-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX; the key is bound to the machine ID, so one key activates one workstation. Volume and fleet licensing is available on request.

Can MRO Pro handle a large spare-parts catalog?

Yes. MRO Pro is built for industrial parts catalogs — including cross-referenced SKUs like bearings, motors, sensors, PLCs, and hydraulic components. See our industrial spare-parts cross-reference for an example of the part-number scale it manages.
