Frequently asked

Practical answers

How do you verify a supplier before quoting?

We check the Chinese business licence and unified social credit code, confirm the production scope matches your part, and audit the site or review recent audit records before we commit to a price.

What payment terms do you recommend?

For a new supplier we recommend 30% deposit with 70% against bill-of-lading copy, settled by T/T. We do not advise paying 100% upfront to a factory you have not audited.

Can you protect our drawings and IP?

Yes. We sign NNN agreements before drawings are shared, and can advise on registering key marks in China. Confidentiality is handled before technical disclosure, not after.

Do you handle customs clearance ourselves?

We prepare documentation, handle commodity inspection where required and coordinate with your forwarder on FOB, or manage the shipment to port on CIF.

Ready to source with confidence?

Send a specification, part number or BOM. You will get a verified quote within 48 hours — including supplier, spec confirmation and landed cost.
